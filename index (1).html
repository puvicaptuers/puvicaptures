Inquiries & Answers

Questions before you step in front of the camera?

Do I need modeling experience?

Not at all. Over 90% of our clients are entrepreneurs, executives, and leaders who have never been photographed professionally before. Our process is entirely hands-on: we guide every angle, shoulder tilt, facial expression, and posture so you never wonder what to do with your hands.

What should I wear?

During our Phase II Strategize consultation, we create a bespoke Wardrobe & Styling Moodboard. We advise on architectural silhouettes, rich textures (wool, silk, linen, cashmere), and timeless neutral palettes that flatter your skin tone and harmonize with your personal brand aesthetic.

Where can I use my photographs?

The Executive Monograph Session includes a full commercial usage license, so your images can be used across your website, press, publications, book sleeves, keynotes, and social channels.

How do I book a session?

Fill in the inquiry form above or contact the studio at puvicaptures@gmail.com. We respond within 24 hours to begin your consultation.

When will I receive my images?

Your hand-retouched master files are delivered within 10 business days of your session, along with a private high-resolution online gallery.
